# Brainpower Education V7 — Deep Lesson Experience

V7 is focused almost entirely on teaching quality. The course paths and question bank from V6 are retained, but the lesson pages have been rebuilt to feel much closer to a real guided mastery course rather than short notes followed by two questions.

## Major lesson changes

Every lesson now contains a longer sequence:

1. **Learning goals** — what the student should be able to do, not just what content is covered.
2. **Big picture** — where the idea fits and how to think about it.
3. **Key lens** — the main pattern-recognition idea for the lesson.
4. **Why the method works** — conceptual or derivational reasoning rather than formula memorisation.
5. **Core relation** — the key formula shown in context.
6. **Worked method** — the original method skeleton, now presented as a deliberate reasoning process.
7. **Full worked example** — detailed, line-by-line examples on the most important Specialist 3/4 lessons.
8. **Exam lens** — common traps and what a strong solution should do.
9. **Guided practice** — the required lesson questions.
10. **Optional depth practice** — three additional topic questions selected from the existing question bank.
11. **Retrieval check** — four short prompts that force the student to explain when/why/how the method works.
12. **Mastery gate** — lesson completion remains locked until all required questions are correct.

## Course-wide depth layer

A new `src/data/lesson-depth-v7.js` file supplies deep teaching content for every course/topic, including:

- conceptual big-picture explanations
- why-the-method-works reasoning
- core ideas
- common mistakes
- exam technique
- cross-topic connections

This means even older V3/V6 lessons now receive a substantially richer learning experience without duplicating the course-sequencing data.
